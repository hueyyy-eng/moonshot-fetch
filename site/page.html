◆Pullback watch

Demand already proven, price since reset: ran ≥3× off its 7-day low, now 40–70% below the highest high in its candle window (up to 30 days), 24h volume ≥30% of its peak day, unique buyers ≥ sellers, rug checks clean. The "second leg" case, made mechanical.

▲Igniting

The third case, and the hardest: moving right now, early enough to matter. Volume in the last 6 hours running ≥1.5× the day's average pace, price up on 6h but not parabolic, 24h not already blown out, and under 3× off its 7-day low. Brand-new pairs with no candle history are allowed in here — that is where early moves actually live.

⚡Momentum

Experimental (13 Oct 2026). The opposite bet to the Shortlist: tokens 1–3 days old on Solana, BSC, Robinhood Chain or Elysium, $500k–2m cap, with buyers between 45% and 90% of traders and no honeypot flag. In the first 23 days of snapshots this profile reached 2× about three times as often as average — and was down 50%+ well over half the time. High variance by design; judge it by its own track record below, not by this description.

»Runners

Experimental (8 Nov 2026). Tokens that already ran 3× or more but are still under 3 days old, with 70–90% of traders buying and trading 10× or more their liquidity. In the back-test this group reached 5× more often than any list (13%), but 9 in 10 later fell by half or more. Trade it fast: half out at 2×, all out within 3 days.

↗Breakouts

Added 11 Nov 2026. Tokens the scanner saw at $200k–$2m in the last 7 days that have now grown past $2m — kept on show for 3 days instead of vanishing. In the back-test (161 tokens, measured from the price when they crossed $2m) 19% doubled again, 7% reached 5× and 4 reached 10× — but 68% ended down 50% or more. A second chance to follow a winner, not a safe one.

◌Early watch

Experimental (11 Nov 2026). Smaller tokens, $200k–$500k, on the core chains: at least 1 day old, more buyers than sellers (but not 90%+), enough liquidity, not collapsing, no wash trading, rug checks not failed. In the back-test, tokens of this size a day before they were found doubled about 20% of the time vs 8% overall — and smaller tokens rug more. Its own list: never mixed into the five lists above and never sent to Telegram until a review says it works.

◇Playbook

The same candidates sorted the way you actually trade them: by type first, then by chain or by narrative. Type is about survival, not size — a token is a leader because it lived through a drawdown and still trades. Sizing and hold period are your framework's, shown here as labels, not as advice.

      ⓘHow to use

      A plain-English guide: what this page is for, a daily checklist, the live scoreboard of how past picks did, and what each part means. Hover any section title or column heading on the page for the same explanations.

      What this dashboard is

      A daily research list of small crypto tokens worth a closer look — not a buy list. Twice a day the scanner checks which blockchains have money and trading moving in, looks at every $500k–$2m token on those chains (plus a separate group of bigger "leaders" above $2m), and keeps the ones that pass a set of safety and "the demand looks real" checks. It then follows every token it ever listed, so you can see whether the method actually works.

      Daily checklist

      1. Check the date at the top. Make sure you are looking at today's scan (GitHub sometimes starts it hours late).
      2. Liquidity flow (step 1): which chains are "hot". Tokens are searched on hot chains, the 6 core chains (including Elysium), and the 3 chains with the biggest 7-day net inflow (tagged top inflow).
      3. Read the five lists: ★ Shortlist (passed every check), ◆ Pullback watch (ran, dipped, buyers still there), ▲ Igniting (moving right now — act fast or skip), ⚡ Momentum (experimental, very new tokens), » Runners (experimental, brand-new tokens still running). Two more below them: ↗ Breakouts (tokens that just grew past $2m, shown for 3 days) and ◌ Early watch (experimental, smaller $200k–$500k tokens). A green second look tag marks a young token whose price kept rising since the previous scan with buyers still in charge. Within each list, start from the highest Score; grey "tracks an asset" tokens sit at the bottom.
      4. For any token you like, check it yourself: hover the gate bars and safety pill to see why it passed, note its type tag, then use Narrative to see whether real people on X are talking about it.
      5. Size it with the Playbook: pot A (safest) gets the biggest share, then B, then C; pot C only in easy markets.
      6. Decide your exit before you buy — a price to take profit and a price to cut the loss. The scoreboard below shows why.
      7. Once a week, look at the scoreboard (Track record, Price tracking, Pattern study) to see whether the picks are earning their keep.
      8. Every other Sunday, open the Review tab: every back-test recalculated on all the data so far, with the last review's numbers in brackets. Change a rule only when the change holds up there.

      Each part of the page

      Before you put money in

      • This page finds conditions, not winners. Most candidates still fail, and many of these tokens go to zero.
      • Only use money you can afford to lose completely, and spread it in small amounts rather than one big bet.
      • Gains on these tokens are often gone within days — have your exit plan before you buy.
      • Igniting and Momentum are the most time-sensitive lists. If the scan ran late, the move may already be over by the time you see it.
      • Nothing on this page is financial advice.

      Limits worth knowing

      • This finds conditions, not winners. Every gate here is a precondition the checklist says precedes a large move. Most candidates still fail. Nothing on this page is a recommendation.
      • Rug data has gaps. RugCheck covers Solana, GoPlus covers the EVM chains, Honeypot.is simulates a real sell on BSC/Base/Arbitrum. Robinhood Chain is listed by GoPlus but returns no holder or authority data, so it is checked from its Blockscout explorer instead — holders with the pool excluded, verified source, mint and owner-control functions — and shown as partial ok. Nothing can simulate a sell there yet. A contract whose source is not verified stays no data, never a false pass.
      • A failed sell simulation is not a honeypot. Uniswap V3/V4 routing makes the simulator revert on some healthy tokens, so a revert is recorded as inconclusive, not a flag.
      • Buy pressure is measured in wallets, not trades. Trade counts are trivially botted; unique buyers and sellers are much harder to fake. Where GeckoTerminal has no wallet counts the gate shows unknown rather than guessing.
      • Tokens under about a day old have no daily candles, so their already-ran check falls back to the 24h move. Marked new pair on the row.
      • Discovery is the real bottleneck, not the gates. Ranking candidates by 24-hour volume structurally favours tokens whose move is already in that number — in one test, 17 of the top 24 tokens trending on 6-hour activity were absent from the scan entirely. The full scan now runs a second pass ranked by 6-hour trending to catch them while they are still moving.
      • The track record is the only honest scoreboard here, and it starts from nothing. Until it has weeks of entries, treat every threshold on this page as an untested guess.
      • Refresh re-prices what is listed, in your browser only, and cannot discover new tokens or refresh perp figures. The twice-daily scan is what discovers new tokens. Nothing you do here is saved.
